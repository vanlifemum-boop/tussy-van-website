How to dispose of correctly - the quick guide

  • The toilet cassette is only emptied at designated chemical toilet stations (toilet bus / camper service) - never in normal toilets, sinks or drains. Then rinse thoroughly and leave the station clean.
  • Greywater (dishwashing and shower water) belongs in the designated floor inlet - not on the meadow, in the forest or the street drain. Even “just dishwashing water” contains fats and surfactants.
  • Fresh water should only be filled at approved taps using your own food-safe hose. Check the local signs to confirm whether the water is suitable for drinking.
  • Information without Guarantee: Equipment can change - check the signs briefly on site.
