How to Dispose of Properly — The Quick Start Guide

  • The toilet cassette is emptied exclusively at designated stations for chemical toilets (toilet bus / camper service) — never in regular toilets, sinks, or gutters. Then rinse thoroughly and leave the station clean.
  • greywater (Rinse and shower water) belongs in the designated ground inlet — not in the meadow, forest, or road gully. Even "only dishwater" contains fats and surfactants.
  • fresh water you fill it at designated outlets — but only with your own food-safe hose. Whether the water is of drinking quality is revealed by the local signage.
  • Information without guarantee: Equipment may change — briefly check the signage on site.
