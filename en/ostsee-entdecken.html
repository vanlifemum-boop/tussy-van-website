Discover the Baltic Sea

From Świnoujście to Pomerania to Piaski: coastal towns, travel ideas, and my personal reports from the Polish Baltic Sea.

November 21, 2026 · from your Tussy

The Polish Baltic Sea deserves its own category: from Świnoujście in the west through Western Pomerania and Pomerania to Piaski on the Vistula Spit, near the border with the Russian territory of Kaliningrad. Here, ideas for further stays combine with my already published travel reports.

From west to east

  1. Świnoujście and Międzyzdroje: Island town, beach, and promenade as a starting point; then the Międzyzdroje pier and the landscape on Wolin.
  2. Rewal, Kołobrzeg, and Darłowo: Combine beach days with city visits. In Kołobrzeg, the harbor and lighthouse are on the list, and in Darłowo, the castle of the Pomeranian dukes.
  3. Ustka and Łeba: Harbor spots for walks, plus a private excursion into the dune landscape near Słowiński National Park. I would leave time for that outside of the pure driving days.
  4. Gdynia, Sopot, and Gdańsk: The Tricity is worth a trip on its own. You can find my personal perspective on Gdansk and the neighboring towns in the linked report.
  5. Krynica Morska and Piaski: Continue on to the Vistula Spit between the Baltic Sea and the Vistula Lagoon. My report from Piaski tells of this special end of my route.

These are travel modules in geographical order, not a plan for a single day. For me, it's worth staying several nights at selected places and allowing enough free beach time in between.

My idea for the coastal trip

I would divide the coast into sections: first West Pomerania, then the middle coast, and finally Dreistadt and Vistula Spit. This way, the selection remains usable even for shorter holidays. If you only have one week, you choose a section. A rainy day can become a city day; in good weather, more time is spent by the sea.

Piaski is the endpoint on the Polish side here. The route does not cross the national border. On site, walks follow the signposted paths and accessible areas.
