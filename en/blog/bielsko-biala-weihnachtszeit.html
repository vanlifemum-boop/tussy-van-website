Christmas time in Bielsko-Biała: my walk through the lights

Lights over the alleys, a radiant Christmas tree, and Reksio in festive dress: my personal Christmas moments from Bielsko-Biała.

November 21, 2026 · from your Tussy

Hello, this is Justine from Tussy Van! Some travel photos instantly take you back into a moment. In my Christmas photos from Bielsko-Biała, it's the lights: high above the alleys, around the windows, and reflected on the wet pavement. It's exactly this warm, festive atmosphere I want to show you.

When the whole alley lights up

In my pictures, you don't need snow to get into the Christmas spirit. The floor gleams after the rain, and the light reflects between the old facades. Bright garlands hang above the street, with colored spheres in between. For me, that's the most beautiful invitation to slow down, to stop, and to look up for a change.

What I really like about BB is this mix: the city has its own architecture and its own everyday life, and during the Christmas season, this warm light settles over it. There doesn't have to be a big program on every corner. Even the path through the decorated streets becomes an experience.

A Christmas tree that transforms the square

The large tree with its red and white motifs is one of my favorite pictures of the evening. Below, gift packages glow, and above them, the sparkle stretches to the top. All around, the houses stand in the dark, and the colors reflect on the square. Moments like these make a winter visit special for me: you look at a familiar urban space and suddenly experience it in a completely different way.

Reindeer, Santa Claus, and a Glowing Reksio

Festive motifs on my walk also include the large reindeer with the sleigh and the cheerful dog with a Christmas hat. Reksio fits perfectly with Bielsko-Biała and its animated story. Of course, it had to be a souvenir photo—with my pink cap in the glow of lights.

My tip for a Christmas visit

I would start the city stroll in daylight and leave enough time until evening. First, discover the houses and squares, warm up in between, and then walk through the illuminated streets once more. With comfortable, weatherproof shoes and without too many fixed appointments, this atmosphere is best enjoyed.

My photos are travel memories from December 2023. They show the decoration during this visit; Setup, lighting, and events may look different in other years. For your appointment, you can find the current cultural program via the official Bielsko-Biała site.

Discover BB even during the day

If you stay longer, you will find in my Bielsko-Biała Travel Report you will find the old town, museums, the OKO animation centre and ideas for the Beskid Mountains. Read why BB is also my Poland recommendation from the Leipzig travel fair in my trade fair contribution.

For me, Bielsko-Biała is a particularly beautiful Poland recommendation during the Christmas season: because of the warm lights, the little discoveries along the way, and the joy that lingers in such travel photos.

See you soon and always have a good trip,
