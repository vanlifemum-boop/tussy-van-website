Gliwice: Old Town, Palm Trees, and a Piece of Technological History

My travel idea for Gliwice: old town, palm trees, and a piece of technical history. With sights and a suggestion for your visit.

November 21, 2026 · from your Tussy

For a city visit with different impressions, I would recommend Gliwice: first small old town alleys, then plants under glass, or a look at the history of the radio.

From the Rynek into the Alleys

The marketplace with the town hall forms a clear starting point. From here, roads lead through the historic city center. For me, that would be the right place to combine the first coffee with a walk and not pack up the camera right after the first shot.

Willa Caro and Radiostacja

The Willa Caro belongs to the museum in Gliwice and provides access to civic history. Radiostacja places a different focus: the broadcast location is closely linked to the staged attack of August 31, 1939. I would deliberately plan both places as separate tours.

A green switch

The Palmiarnia, the palm house, complements the city stroll with a world of plants under glass. That's a nice idea for a day when the weather doesn't look like a long walk. Current visit times and tickets should be added to the checklist before the trip.

This is how I would plan the visit

Old town and lunch break first, then palm house or museum. I would plan the Radiostacja as a separate detour and check the connection between the locations beforehand, instead of just stopping briefly everywhere.

Traveling by camper

I would park the car at a suitable starting point and plan the tours from there. For the night, choose a specifically suitable campsite or pitch. My Poland map helps with further orientation.

Plan further

This travel article combines official local information with my suggestion for a visit. You can find current opening hours, guided tours, and bookings via the Official city portrait of Gliwice on Śląskie Travel.
