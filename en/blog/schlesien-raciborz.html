Racibórz: Piast Castle, Oder and old city walls

My travel idea for Racibórz: Piast castle, or old city walls. With sights and a suggestion for your visit.

November 21, 2026 · from your Tussy

I would plan Racibórz as the southwestern component of a Silesia trip. The city on the Oder combines historic buildings with opportunities for a green afternoon.

To Piast Castle

The Piast Castle with its Gothic chapel is the first stop for me. The chapel dedicated to Saint Thomas Becket is one of the special architectural elements. Anyone who wants to see the interiors should check the current viewing and book a separate appointment for it.

Walls and Marketplace

Remnants of the city fortifications and the former prison tower reveal the older city structure. Around the market are other churches and historic sites. I would start with a small selection and decide along the way where I want to stay longer.

Into the Arboretum

The Bramy Morawskiej Arboretum offers a completely different focus with forest paths and garden areas. After a morning full of buildings, this would be my suggestion for more time outside. I would plan the city and arboretum as separate stages with separate directions.

This is how I would plan the visit

The castle and the center form the first part. After a lunch break, either a museum visit or the arboretum follows. If you only have half a day, it's better to stay with the historic city instead of shortening two programs.

Traveling by camper

I would park the car at a suitable starting point and plan the tours from there. For the night, choose a specifically suitable campsite or pitch. My Poland map helps with further orientation.

Plan further

This travel article combines official local information with my suggestion for a visit. You can find current opening hours, guided tours, and bookings via the Official city portrait of Racibórz on Śląskie Travel.
