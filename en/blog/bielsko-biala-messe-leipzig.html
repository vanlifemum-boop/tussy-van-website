My Poland recommendation from Leipzig: Bielsko-Biała

November 20, 2026 · Poland · approx. 3 min. reading time · from your Tussy

Tourism & Caravaning Leipzig 2026

18.–November 22, 2026 · daily 10 a.m.–6 p.m.

Leipzig Trade Fair · Poland: Hall 4, Booth 4D21

City strolls, cartoon characters and the Beskid Mountains on the doorstep – Bielsko-Biała belongs on my Poland travel list.

My trade fair highlight from the Polish booth at Touristik & Caravaning in Leipzig: Bielsko-Biała! A city in southern Poland that combines culture and mountains. Exactly the mix I want to recommend for your next trip to Poland.

In 2026, Bielsko-Biała becomes the first city to hold the title Polish Capital of Culture. What is meant is the Polish title – and this is a nice occasion to take a closer look: the old town, the castle of the Sułkowski princes, the magnificent facades, and the cultural offerings of the city.

It's especially exciting for families: Bolek and Lolek as well as Reksio are at home here. On the character route, you encounter the cartoon hero in the cityscape; at the interactive animation center OKO, you can discover how drawings become moving stories.

And then out into the countryside? Around Szyndzielnia and the Beskids, a city visit can be combined with a nature day. My tip: Plan more than just a short stopover for BB.

Poland faces itself from November 18 to 22, 2026 at the Leipzig Trade Fair. Bielsko-Biała is present at the Polish joint booth: Hall 4, Booth 4D21.
