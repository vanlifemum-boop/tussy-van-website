Goodbye Cologne, hello Europe: 6 weeks, 4 courses, and the pure feeling of life

How it all began · approx. 7 min. reading time · from your Tussy

Hello, this is Justine from Tussy Van! 💛 By mid-August, the moment had come: the van was packed, the key was in the ignition lock, and in Cologne it was simply — Departure! Ahead of me lay six weeks of absolute freedom, which would take me across the continent: from the Alps through the cultural metropolises of Italy all the way up to the Polish Baltic Sea. I was on the road until early October — a journey I will never forget for the rest of my life.

It was the perfect time. A late summer straight out of a picture book, which has blessed me well into October with beautiful weather, countless stories and images that you will carry in your heart forever.

Lake Constance and a gear with character

My first big stop took me south to the beautiful Lake Constance. I stopped in the idyllic Langenargen and in the fascinating Zeppelin Museum in nearby Friedrichshafen. Anyone who has ever been there knows how impressive these giants of the skies are.

But even then, it became clear that this road trip would be a real adventure. My upgraded T4 — back then still my first bus, powered by a powerful V6 engine — suddenly developed a personality of its own: the fifth gear completely said goodbye. What do you do there? Turn it around? Absolutely not! I had four others. And so I'm just with the maximum four courses across all of Europe Tucked. Who needs fifth gear when you want to celebrate the slowness of travel anyway?

🔩 A brief review: That was still the time of my very first T4, the silver VR6. How this era began—and how it later ended bitterly—I recount in "The T4 VR6 as an everyday camper" and "The Bitter End of My VR6".

Bella Italia: From the coast to the cultural gems

With four gears, we crossed the mountains. Italy welcomed me with open arms and this incomparable ease. My route was an absolute dream:

  • Genoa — the proud port city with its narrow alleys.
  • Pisa — of course, the obligatory view of the Leaning Tower was not to be missed.
  • Florence — the heart of Tuscany, art and cuisine on every corner.
  • Rome — the Eternal City has overwhelmed me with its millennia-old history.
  • Venice — the magical city in the water as the crowning, romantic conclusion to my Italian adventure.

Course Northeast: Culture and Sea in Poland

After the Mediterranean atmosphere, I changed direction and headed for Poland. The diversity this continent offers is simply unimaginable. The first stop was the historic pilgrimage site Częstochowa with the famous Black Madonna (my report on this →), followed by the beautiful Krakow — a city full of life, young culture, and impressive architecture (my Krakow highlights →). But it pulled me even higher, following the call of the North, all the way to the Baltic Sea coast Gdańsk (Gdańsk). You read my honest view of Danzig, the Tricity, and the amber gold here →

The true camper happiness

What lingers after six weeks and thousands of kilometers? It's not just the famous sights. It's the feeling of sliding open the T4's sliding door in the morning and gazing straight out onto the sparkling sea. It is the tranquility when you stand in the middle of the forest in the evening, breathe in the scent of nature, and gaze at the silhouette of the mountains in the evening light.

This trip showed me how rich this continent is in landscapes, cultures, and impressions. And that for the greatest happiness in life, sometimes you only need four courses and the right support. Europe, you were beautiful!
