From van to colossus: Why my T4 is getting too small for me (and the next big adventure awaits!)

July 2026 · 5 min. reading time · from your Tussy

There are those moments in life when you sit in the morning with a cup of coffee in the sliding door of your van, look out at the sea – and involuntarily pull your knees because the cooler box is in the way again.

Don't get me wrong: I love my T4. He took me over Alpine passes, to lonely beaches, and through sleepless, beautiful thunderstorm nights. It's not a car, it's a way of life. But let's not kid ourselves: anyone who lives, travels, or even spends a rainy weekend in the T4 is constantly running Tetris on wheels. One wrong move, and the clean laundry ends up in coffee grounds.

After years of "head-in-focus" and "compact thinking," I made a decision. I don't want to shrink anymore. I want to go big. And in the truest sense of the word.

The moment the world became too small

The initial spark came a few weeks ago at a parking lot in Scandinavia. Next to me was a converted, all-wheel-drive expedition truck. An absolute monster of a vehicle. While I tried to hang my wet jacket in the rain so it wouldn't flood the bed, the neighbor felt like I was sitting in a cozy old apartment on wheels – with standing height, a fixed bed, and a kitchen that truly deserves the name.

That's when it clicked. Why always restrict? Why not take the dream of a mobile home a step further? A whole One size larger.

Mission: Truck Driver's License 🚛

The plan is in place, but before the dream comes reality – and the bureaucracy. To legally drive such a large vehicle across the country, my regular driver's license is obviously not enough at all.

For me, that means: Back to driving school! In the coming weeks, I'll be at school, studying theory, and trying to maneuver a ton-heavy monster through city traffic accident-free. The idea of soon being behind the wheel of a real mummy fills me with a hell of a lot of respect – but the anticipation outweighs the anticipation.

The big question: build it yourself or buy it ready-made?

Once the bill is in the bag, the real adventure begins. And honestly? I have absolutely no idea what happens next. The gears in my head keep turning nonstop, and I swing daily between two extremes:

  • Option A: The total do-it-yourself madness. Buying an old fire truck, a THW vehicle, or a decommissioned Bundeswehr truck. Gutting everything, insulating, sawing, hammering, and building my own loft on wheels from scratch. Sweat, tears, and pride included.
  • Option B: The finished nest. Buy something solid, already developed. Get in, turn the key, and set off. Less craftsmanship thrill, but faster to the destination.

What will it be in the end? We'll see. I take you with me every step of the way.

Join me on this journey!

This is the starting signal for a completely new chapter. No idea if I'll fail the first theory test, if I'll take a traffic light when parking with the driving school truck, or if I'll end up in front of an empty truck and despair. But that's exactly what makes it, isn't it?

Do you have experience with the truck driver's license yourselves, or do you perhaps already drive an "expedition vehicle"? What would you do: build it completely yourself or buy it ready-made? Be sure to write it in the Comments on YouTube!

Get ready – it's going to be big!
