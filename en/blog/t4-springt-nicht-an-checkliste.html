T4 won't start? My 10-Point Checklist

May 2026 · 10 min. reading time · from your Tussy

Parking in the Pyrenees, 7 a.m., keys in — and nothing. Click. Quiet. Three years and a few mishaps later, I go through this list before I even think about the phone. In 8 out of 10 cases, I continue an hour later.

⚠️ Safety first: Ignition off when tweaking the electrics, handbrake on, and everything related to fuel: no open flame. Unsure? Then dear professional — the list still helps you narrow down the problem (and have a say in the workshop).

First listen: What happens during the key turning?

Nothing at all? → points 1–4. Click, but no organ? → points 4–6. Organ but not starting? → points 7–10.

The checklist

  1. Battery terminals: Loose or white-green corroded? Unclip, clean with a wire brush, tighten. Solves surprisingly many "total failures."
  2. Battery voltage: Below 12.0 V it gets tight, below 11.5 V nothing starts. A multimeter (€10) belongs in every van.
  3. Mass tape: Engine body, often forgotten, often rusted through. Visual inspection!
  4. Ignition lock contact: If the lock is worn out, bypassing the starter helps as a test — or first: relieve the steering wheel lock and use the key "with feeling."
  5. Starter click: Magnetic switch clicks but doesn't turn? Classic: Coal worn out. The famous "hammer trick" (gently tapping the starter housing while someone starts) gets you all the way to the workshop.
  6. Fuses & Relays: With the T4, Relay 109 (Engine control unit) THE usual suspect — to have as a replacement in the glove compartment for 15 €. My relay is now called "Horst II".
  7. Diesel: Wait for pre-glowing. Warning light broken? Check glow plugs (voltage tester). In cold weather, that makes all the difference.
  8. Fuel then? Fuel gauges lie, especially when parked uphill. And: After changing the filter, the system must be vented.
  9. Negative pressure/side flap (diesel): If the parking device doesn't open up, there's no fuel. Remove the hose, visual inspection, and if necessary, temporarily fix it.
  10. Immobilizer: Is the warning light blinking? Test the second key — sometimes only the transponder is tired.

My breakdown emergency kit

  • Multimeter, wire brush, contact spray
  • Relay 109 ("Horst II"), fuse range
  • Jump starter cable + power bank with jump starter function
  • Workshop manual offline as PDF on your phone
