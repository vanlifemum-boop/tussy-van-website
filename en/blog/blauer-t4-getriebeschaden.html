Workshop Story

Now the blue one has to go to the workshop too — 3rd and 5th gear are gone

Current Workshop Story · approx. 5 min. reading time · from your Tussy

After the red, I thought I had used up my breakdown quota for now. The Blue apparently had other plans. This time it's the transmission: the third and fifth gears no longer work. So it's back to workshop instead of onward journey.

Two gears missing — but the cause is still unclear

If individual gaits fail, the temptation is to immediately look for a diagnosis on the internet. Linkage, adjustment, bearing, or something inside the transmission itself — theoretically, various things come into play. However, as long as the vehicle is not properly inspected, any precise claim would be advisable. And that's exactly what I don't want.

What I can say for sure: The third and fifth gears are not usable. Therefore, normal and reliable driving is not an option for me. The blue one got onto the tow truck and needs to be examined.

The old feeling is immediately back

An older T4 is more than romance and beautiful pitches. It requires attention, maintenance, and sometimes a lot of patience. When I saw the blue one on the tow truck, the memory of the red one was instantaneous. Of course, I know that a gearbox failure doesn't automatically mean the end of the vehicle. Nevertheless, the fear of such experiences runs deep.

🔧 The current status: 3rd and 5th gear do not work. The exact cause and extent of repair will only be determined after the workshop diagnosis. Until then, I deliberately don't mention costs or supposedly defective parts.

Why I show this side of Vanlife

On Instagram, you often see the finished camp by the lake. The tow truck fits less well into the dream setting, but it belongs just as well. If you travel with an old bus, you don't have to be able to repair everything yourself. However, you should recognize when continuing is no longer a good idea and be honest with the condition of the vehicle.

Now I'm waiting for the diagnosis. I hope the Blue will soon find all the hallways again. And if not, that part of the story — unvarnished, without invented heroism and with the tools where it belongs.

See you soon and always have a good trip,
