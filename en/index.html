About me

Hello, I'm Justine.

Bulli driver, self-mechanic, sun worshipper

Made up, self-sufficient, and with my VW T4 across Europe. I've been traveling in my own van since 2020 — but I've already soaked up camping with my mother's milk. It all started with a silver T4 Caravelle (built 1998, VR6) — after an engine failure came the red one, and finally my current companion, the blue one: royal blue under yellow VW stickers. I'll show you traveling from my perspective: honestly, independently, and with mascara on the dipstick.
