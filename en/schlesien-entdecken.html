This category follows the present-day Śląskie Voivodeship. This also includes places in Zagłębie and the region around Częstochowa; historical landscape boundaries run differently. Personal experiences and researched suggestions for further visits are clearly visible in the article.

A route to your taste

For city and industrial culture, I would combine Katowice, Gliwice, Zabrze, and Tarnowskie Góry. Those who prefer to connect alleys, castles, and mountain landscapes can start with Bielsko-Biała, Pszczyna, Cieszyn, Wisła, and Żywiec. The other articles give you additional building blocks for your own route. It's not a call to pack everything into one week.

You can find the official basis at Śląskie Travel; for BB directly on pskbb.slaskie.travel. For travel planning, there is also my interactive Poland map.
