Discover Masuria

Travel ideas between lakes, forests, and port towns: Giżycko, Mikołajki, Ruciane-Nida, and other destinations for the Poland series.

November 21, 2026 · from your Tussy

Water, forest, and small places where a journey can sometimes slow down: This is where my column for Masuria begins. To get started, I've put together travel ideas around the Great Lakes. From this, you can combine individual stays; it doesn't have to be a complete round trip.

Morąg: a museum stop on the way to the lakes

In the Dohna Palace, the Museum of Upper Prussia displays art and stories from the region. Morąg is located in the historic highlands, west of the inner Masuria – a great travel idea for getting there or for your own excursion.

Giżycko: Water and History

The Boyen Fortress, the swing bridge on the Łuczański Canal, and the former water tower are destinations for a city visit. I would plan a morning for history followed by time by the water. To me, that sounds like a good mix if not every vacation day is just about driving.

Official information about Giżycko

Mikołajki: stay at the harbor

The promenade at Lake Mikołajki and the harbor form the starting point. Excursion trips lead, among other places, towards Śniardwy. I would only decide on a boat tour after the weather and the actual schedule and keep the rest of the day free. A longer walk by the water can also be part of the entire program.

Official information about Mikołajki

Ruciane-Nida and the forests

The official cycling circuit around Lake Bełdany connects Ruciane-Nida and Mikołajki with the forest landscape of the Puszcza Piska. It is a suggestion for a dedicated cycling day. In advance, I would check the route, surface, and any required ferry connection and adjust the stage to my own condition.

Bełdany cycling circuit with detailed route description

Other places for the Masurian series

Węgorzewo, Ryn, Mrągowo, and Pisz complement the region. Together with Giżycko, Mikołajki, and Ruciane-Nida, they lie on the large Masurian cycle route. This provides orientation for later excursions; it is a bike path and not a driving instruction for the camper.

The Masurian Cycle Loop at the official regional portal

This is how I would start

For a first trip, I would choose two locations: one in the Giżycko area and one further south. In between, there is a day without a fixed program. For the camper, I arrange a suitable overnight spot in advance, and for water activities, the specific offer. Further personal experiences and detailed local reports can gradually add to this section.
