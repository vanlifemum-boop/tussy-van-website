Campervan, semi-integrated, alcove or integrated?

Guide · 6 min. reading time

⏱️ No time? Here is the summary

Campervan/Van: compact, agile, unobtrusive – perfect for solo or as a couple and for freestanding. Semi-integrated: the golden middle ground with a real bathroom and kitchen. Alcoves: the family car with a bed above the cab and plenty of sleeping places. Integrated: the luxury class with panoramic windows and maximum sense of space – but expensive. There is no the the perfect camper, just the one that fits your life.

Hello, this is Justine from Tussy Van! 💛 If you're just diving into the wonderful world of caravanning, you'll quickly be bombarded with terms like campervan, panel van, alcove, or integrated vehicle. Suddenly, everyone uses these words as a matter of course – but hardly anyone explains exactly what lies behind them. Don't worry: I'll take a closer look at the four types for you – straightforward, practical, and honest.

1. Campervan (panel van) — the flexible home on wheels

A campervan is a classic van that has been lovingly transformed into a cozy home on wheels. Usually, models like the Fiat Ducato, Ford Transit, or Mercedes Sprinter serve as the base — the living space remains completely within the original vehicle dimensions. (By the way, my blue T4 also belongs to this family, just one size smaller.) 😉

Why campervans are so popular:

  • Extremely agile — drives almost like a normal car.
  • Fits standard parking lots and tight city centers.
  • Inconspicuous — a huge advantage if you like to stand alone or "wild."
  • Fuel and maintenance costs remain manageable.

The downside: Space is limited. Sometimes it's said: either a larger kitchen or more spacious bathroom. But if you love dynamism, spontaneous stops, and flexible plan changes, a campervan is worth its weight in gold.

2. Semi-integrated motorhome — the ideal compromise

When the camping scene simply refers to a "motorhome," it usually refers to a semi-integrated meant. The original cab will be preserved and combined with a wider, custom-built residential structure.

  • Significantly more freedom of movement and sense of space than in a campervan.
  • Space for a full bathroom and a comfortable kitchen.
  • Good insulation — comfortable even in the colder months.
  • Still comfortable length and stable handling.

The perfect choice if you want tangible comfort but don't want to maneuver a huge "ship" around the campsite.

3. Alcove — the timeless classic for families

You can immediately recognize the alcove by its distinctive "nose" above the cab. This cozy hump almost always contains a permanently installed, spacious double bed.

  • Maximum sleeping places on a compact footprint.
  • Room layout perfect for 4 to 6 people.
  • No tedious remodeling — the bed upstairs is always ready.

What you should keep in mind: The design provides higher air resistance — and thus more consumption. In strong winds, in tight city centers, or on winding mountain roads, a bit more attention is required. If you're traveling with children and prioritize function over design, the alcove is unbeatable.

4. Integrated motorhome — pure luxury on wheels

The top class. With the integrated vehicle, there is no longer a commercial vehicle cab — the entire vehicle is designed from the ground up as a standalone motorhome.

  • Gigantic sense of space and maximum use of space.
  • Huge panoramic windshield with a breathtaking view.
  • First-class noise and thermal insulation.
  • Living comfort at the level of a modern holiday apartment.

The catch: The purchase price, dimensions, and maintenance costs are quite significant (just replacing the huge windshield can get really expensive). Ideal for anyone who spends a lot of time in the motorhome or simply doesn't want to compromise.

Conclusion — which type suits you?
  • Campervan: for mobile, spontaneous adventurers — ideal solo or as a couple.
  • Semi-integrated: the golden mean for couples seeking comfort on long journeys.
  • Alcoves: the best choice for families where spaciousness and functionality matter.
  • Integrated: the maximum luxury, space, and comfort.
There is no the one Perfect motorhome — just the one that perfectly fits your lifestyle!
