About me

Hello, this is Justine.

… the Tussy from the Tussy Van

Actually, I just wanted an affordable car for the summer. What I got: a home, a hobby, a best friend on four wheels — and a YouTube channel.

Camping? That's me.

I've been traveling in my own van since 2020 — but camping I basically absorbed with mother's milk. For me, it was never just a hobby I eventually chose: It's been in me since childhood. I am camping ❤️ — and the van simply gave wheels to that feeling.

Why actually "Tussy"?

When I wanted to create my Instagram and a Gmail address, "Tussi" with an i had long been taken. So I quickly made a "the" from it — Tussy. What I only realized later: My real last name also has a Y. For as long as I can remember, I've said at every administrative visit "... with Ypsilon, not with i!". That my van name now needs exactly the same explanation was the sign for me: The Y stays. Made up — and then checked the oil level

Yes, I'm made up. I love cosmetics and see no reason to give up my femininity at the barrier to the campsite. For many, that's a contradiction: If you wear mascara, you can't screw it, can you? Yes. I check the oil level, repair my van, and explain the autonomous onboard technology — and I like to do that with perfect eyeliner. I've made the insult "Tussi" my favorite trademark: If someone at the parking lot says "over there stands Tussy with its van," everyone immediately knows who is meant — and no longer in a derogatory way.

Three T4s, one passion

It all started with a silver 1998 VW T4 Caravelle with a VR6 engine — my first self-modified camper. A true all-rounder: sleeping sideways in the back, still room in the front for friends, six of us on spontaneous outings. With it, I took my biggest road trip to date — six weeks from Cologne across Europe, from Italy to the Polish Baltic Sea, all the time with only four gears, because the fifth had said goodbye at Lake Constance. After just one year and a disastrous workshop visit, the VR6 engine was unfortunately a total loss. Give up? Never. This was followed by the Red — and finally my faithful companion today: the blue, royal blue, and covered all over with yellow VW stickers. So colorful that children wave in parking lots and even customs officers smile. Over Alpine passes, to lonely beaches, and all the way to Scandinavia, Vanlife has now taken me — free, honest, and a little cheeky.

I'm currently getting my truck driver's license and dreaming of my own expedition vehicle with standing height and a proper kitchen.
